1 · Connect Wi-Fi

Select your Wi-Fi and enter its password. Then set up your Owlet account.

You are using the “owlanzi” setup Wi-Fi. If needed, open 192.168.4.1. This hotspot briefly closes while connecting.

Leave blank for an open network. Wi-Fi credentials are stored separately from your Owlet account.

Set up Wi-Fi again

Opens the “owlanzi” hotspot for 5 minutes and disconnects the clock from home Wi-Fi. Alternatively, hold the middle button for 6 seconds, then release.

2 · Owlet account

Your credentials stay on the clock. TC002 fetches Owlet directly; your computer is not needed afterwards.

App updates

App version—
Available version—

The clock asks owlanzi.com for a new version once a day. The model and installed app version are sent for daily counts; checks and downloads are counted without a device identifier. Installation requires your confirmation.

Wi-Fi, Owlet credentials and all settings are preserved. The display pauses briefly while the app restarts.

The OTA starter needs to be installed locally once before updates can be installed.
